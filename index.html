🫨 Wiggly

A cute floating desktop companion for macOS that holds a countdown timer for your to-do list — with a Pomodoro tab, a calendar that overlays your real Google and Apple Calendar events, and optional cross-device sync.

macOS

What's inside

  • To-Do List Paste tasks in from a spreadsheet, set a priority and duration, and start the timer straight from the list.
  • Floating gadget An always-on-top companion that holds the running timer, so you always know what's next without switching windows.
  • Pomodoro Classic 25/5/15 focus cycles that auto-advance, with a notification at every switch.
  • Timer & Stopwatch A standalone countdown or a simple stopwatch, whenever a task isn't the point.
  • Calendar Drag tasks onto a real hourly grid — and see your actual Google and Apple Calendar busy times laid over it (Pro).
  • Cross-device sync Sign in on another Mac and your to-do list is right there (Pro).
