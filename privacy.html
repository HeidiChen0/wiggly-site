Privacy Notice

Wiggly's free features run entirely on your own device — your to-do list, timers, and reminders are never sent anywhere.

If you upgrade to Wiggly Pro, your task data syncs to our database (hosted by Supabase) so it can be shared across your devices, and your email is used to identify your account. Payment details are handled entirely by Paddle.com — Wiggly never sees your card information.

If you connect Google Calendar, we read your calendar's busy/free times to show them in the app; we do not store or share your calendar contents beyond what's needed to display them to you.

Questions: contact us through the app or the email associated with your purchase receipt.
